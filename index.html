FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does a project take?

Most websites launch in 1–3 weeks. Mobile apps and custom platforms typically run 4–10 weeks depending on scope. We give you a firm timeline before we start.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We usually split projects into a deposit and milestone payments so it's easy on your cash flow.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Absolutely. We can build on a CMS and train your team, or handle updates for you on a support plan — your choice.

What do you need from me to start?

Just your goals and any existing brand materials. We'll guide the rest — content, structure and design — from there.
